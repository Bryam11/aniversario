# Moonlit sunflower and tulip garden

## Goal
Refine the fullscreen floral reveal into a richer moonlit garden with sunflower/tulip variety and restrained floating hearts while keeping the scene romantic, legible, and performant.

## Scope
- `index.html`
- `assets/css/styles.css`
- `assets/js/main.js`

## Plan
- [ ] Replace the sun-like decorative orb with a crescent moon.
- [ ] Mix sunflower blooms with tulip-inspired yellow flowers across depth layers.
- [ ] Add a small number of subtle floating hearts.
- [ ] Improve scene layering so message, footer, and music control do not compete.
- [ ] Preserve accessibility, reduced motion, and existing close behavior.

## Non-goals
- Do not use heavy particle systems, canvas, or autoplay.
- Do not obscure the message or controls.
- Do not change the romantic copy or add trip details.
